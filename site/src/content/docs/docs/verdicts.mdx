`apmx audit` asks one question about a delivery: is this receipt intact,
from an approved factory, with passing checks, bound to these files, and
signed by a signer your policy trusts? It answers in rows, then in one final
line. Read the final line first.

## Three verdicts

| Verdict | Exit | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VALID | 0 | Everything checked is consistent. |
| INVALID | 1 | Something was checked and is wrong. |
| INCOMPLETE | 2 | Something could not be checked. Never a pass. |

The reason after INVALID or INCOMPLETE is the first row that failed or could
not be checked.

## The rows

<AuditLadder />

Each row prints one symbol: `[+]` pass, `[x]` fail, `[!]` warning or not
checked, `[i]` information or not evaluated.

<Badge text="Since the next release" variant="note" size="small" /> Eight
rows, named as above. v0.7.1 prints nine: the schema checks are a separate
Standards row after Integrity, and Dependencies is named Ingredients.

## Two kinds of VALID

- **`VALID (content-bound; not authenticated)`.** The receipt is intact and
  bound to these files, but nobody your policy trusts signed it. It binds
  content, not identity: anyone who can write the files can write a
  self-consistent unsigned receipt, and it still passes. Only
  `factories.allow` and a trusted signer constrain that.
- **`VALID (authenticated: <workflow>)`.** A trusted signer your policy names
  in `factories.signers` signed a verification summary of exactly these
  receipt lines, covering these files.

## Warnings that keep VALID

These print `[!]` and leave the verdict VALID:

- **Dependencies not audited offline**: `not audited (offline): ...`. Your
  gate audits them online.
- **Unsigned, and the policy does not require a signature**:
  `unsigned: proves content binding, not who ran it`, or
  `not authenticated: policy names no factories.signers`.
- **Signers configured but checked offline, without `require_signed`**:
  `signature not checked (offline)`.
- **`factories.enforcement: warn`**: Factory, Revocation and Identity
  violations print `[!]` instead of `[x]`, for a staged rollout.

A signer pinned only by ref (or not pinned) still passes Identity, with a
note asking you to pin `workflow_sha`.

## When INCOMPLETE is the right answer

INCOMPLETE means APMX could not check something it was asked to. It fails
closed:

- **The policy cannot be loaded**: a typo, a bad digest, an unreachable
  `extends`. A policy that cannot be loaded is never treated as a pass.
- **Dependencies could not be checked**: the bundled APM is missing, or the
  packages could not be reinstalled (network, authentication, package
  access).
- **A required signature could not be checked**: with `require_signed`,
  `--offline`, a missing delivered file to look it up by, or no `gh` CLI.
- **Any pull request gate offline**, or a gate whose policy names no
  `factories.signers`.

`--offline` is not a pass for signatures: with `require_signed`, and always in
a pull request, the verdict is INCOMPLETE.

## What VALID does not mean

- **Not correctness.** A receipt is evidence of how a change was made, not a
  guarantee that the code is correct. Checks are the bar: code that passes
  weak checks still passes.
- **Not a sandbox.** The run was not confined.
- **Not permission to merge**, unless your policy and your gate say so. See
  [auto-merge factory PRs](/apmx/docs/accept-prs/).
